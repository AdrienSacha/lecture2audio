lecture2audio

Turn a lecture into notes you can hear.

Drop a recording, a transcript or slides. Everything the teacher taught stays in; only the noise goes.

Drop your lecture here Recording, transcript, slides (.pdf, .pptx), glossary. A whole folder works too.

    The provider that writes and checks the notes.
    Used for this run only. Never saved.
    Saved in ~/.lecture2audio.env, readable only by you.
    Language
    Voice the script. Off stops at the written notes.
    Fill what the lecture leaves out, from sources whose quotes are checked.
    Also design one thing to build, step by step, as a PDF.
    Optional. The folder of your vault; the notes go in a sub-folder.
    Advanced
    Leave empty for the provider's default.
    A different model is less inclined to approve the first.
    For a compatible API or a local server.

    Working on your lecture

    You can leave this page open. A run that stops can be started again and continues where it was.
